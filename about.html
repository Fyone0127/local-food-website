Kuching food culture

A city where breakfast, coffee, noodles, and late-night bites all have a story.

Kuching's food scene is shaped by Sarawakian, Malay, Chinese, Indian, Dayak, Indonesian, Western, and cafe influences. From kopitiam breakfasts to riverside dinners, the city makes eating feel local, casual, and full of variety.

Local areas

Food is spread across Kuching's familiar streets.

Padungan, India Street, Main Bazaar, Green Hill, Ban Hock, Satok, and the Waterfront each have their own mix of cafes, kopitiams, restaurants, bakeries, and late-night spots.
